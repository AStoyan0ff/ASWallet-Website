Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the ASWallet presentation website or use its interactive demo. The demo is a simulated experience and does not use real accounts, payments or financial information.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the public website available at aswallet.eu and its interactive demo. It describes the current presentation website, not a live banking or payment service.

Demo notice: ASWallet's interactive demo operates entirely with fictional people, transactions and balances. Do not enter real financial, payment or personal information into the demo fields.

2. Information we handle

The website does not provide account registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, card details, bank account details or identity documents through its current interface.

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic or security logs.

3. Interactive demo data

Information entered into the demo is used only to simulate wallet actions in your browser. This may include fictional recipient names, amounts, payment references and notes.

  • No real payment is initiated.
  • No bank, card network or payment processor is contacted.
  • Demo balances and transactions do not represent real funds.
  • Refreshing or resetting the demo may remove simulated activity.

4. Browser storage

The website currently uses browser storage for two limited purposes:

  • Local storage saves demo preferences such as balance visibility, selected currency, compact dashboard mode and notification settings.
  • Session storage keeps a short ASky Assistant conversation history during the current browser session.

This information remains in your browser and can be removed by resetting the demo, clearing the ASky conversation, deleting site data through your browser settings or using a private browsing session.

5. External services and links

ASWallet loads font files from Google Fonts. When these resources are requested, the external provider may receive technical request information according to its own privacy practices.

The website also contains links to GitHub and other ASWallet project resources. Once you follow an external link, the destination service's own terms and privacy policy apply.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to keep the presentation website safe and stable. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because the demo is not intended for real information, you should never submit passwords, card numbers, banking credentials or other sensitive information through it.

7. Your choices

You can control the locally stored website information by:

  • using the demo's reset option;
  • clearing the ASky Assistant conversation;
  • clearing storage for aswallet.eu through your browser settings;
  • blocking browser storage or external font requests through browser controls.

Blocking these features may prevent some preferences, assistant history or visual assets from working as expected.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website gains new functionality, connects to backend services or changes how information is handled. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the ASWallet project can currently be submitted through the ASWallet Website repository .
